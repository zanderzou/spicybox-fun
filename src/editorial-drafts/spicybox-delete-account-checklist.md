**To delete a SpicyBox account, begin with the deletion address printed in its [Privacy Policy, section 9](https://spicy-box.com/assets/files/sb/SpicyBoxPrivacyPolicy-new.pdf): `https://spicy-box.com/delete`.** Prepare first: the policy describes permanent deletion affecting the account, credits and content, with limited legal-record exceptions. Do not treat the final confirmation as a trial you can undo.

This guide checks the documented route and helps you prepare a removal request. We did not delete a customer account, inspect the service's storage systems or verify the authenticated confirmation screen. Labels and additional verification steps may change.

## Choose the action that matches your problem

If you only want to stop a future charge, first resolve the subscription or payment arrangement. If you want the service account removed, use its account-removal process. If your concern is a shared computer, also address the browser and files on that computer.

These are separate questions. One confirmation should not be assumed to answer all three.

| Your goal | What to establish before acting |
| --- | --- |
| Stop recurring billing | Where the renewal is managed, whether it is canceled, and the effective date |
| Remove the service account | Which account is signed in and what the deletion confirmation covers |
| Remove files from a device | Which files were downloaded, where they were saved, and whether copies were synced elsewhere |
| Resolve an incorrect charge | Which transaction is disputed and what evidence is needed before account access disappears |

The [Terms, payment and refund sections](https://spicy-box.com/assets/files/sb/SpicyBoxTermsofUse.pdf), take a restrictive position on refunds, subject to applicable law. Account deletion should not be understood as an automatic refund request or a guarantee that money will be returned.

## Prepare a small record before losing account access

Save only what you need for your own records. More copies are not always better when privacy is your reason for leaving.

1. **Identify the account.** Note the sign-in method and account identifier. If you use multiple email addresses or browser profiles, check which account is currently open.
2. **Record billing evidence.** Keep the relevant receipt, renewal or cancellation confirmation, and transaction date. A concise text record is often more useful than an unredacted screenshot containing unrelated information.
3. **Resolve outstanding questions.** If a charge or support request is still open, ask what information will be needed after removal. Do not assume deleted account details can be reconstructed later.
4. **Decide what to retain locally.** Keep only files you are entitled to retain and actually want. Avoid creating an extra archive merely because a delete button is available.
5. **Separate the desired outcomes.** Write down whether you want billing stopped, the account removed, particular local copies removed, or all three. This makes an incomplete confirmation easier to recognize.

For example, a person may have a saved receipt in email, a file in Downloads and an active session on a second computer. Removing the service account does not, by itself, establish what happened to those independent copies or sessions. They need their own check.

## Read the final confirmation in your own session

Type the documented address yourself or follow it from the policy linked by the service. Verify that the browser is on `spicy-box.com`, rather than trusting a similar brand name in a search result or an unsolicited support message.

If authentication is requested, use the account you intend to remove. Before the last action, read the displayed consequence, account identity and any verification requirement. Stop if the identity is wrong or if the screen promises something materially different from the linked policy; ask the service to clarify first.

After confirming, retain the acknowledgment and its date if one is provided. Avoid submitting repeated deletion requests while a confirmation is pending. If there is no clear result, describe exactly what happened to support: the route used, time, account identifier and displayed message. Never send a password, one-time sign-in code or unnecessary personal image to someone claiming to help.

## What you can check afterward

The policy says content is removed on deletion and identifies exceptions for certain non-content records. That is the provider's published position. A signed-out screen, empty gallery or failed sign-in does not independently demonstrate erasure from every backend system.

For a practical record, distinguish:

- **Observed:** the acknowledgment you received and what your account session now shows.
- **Stated:** what the policy or a written support reply says was removed.
- **Unverified:** storage and processing you cannot inspect yourself.

[Clearing browser cookies and site data](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox) addresses browser-held information. It is a separate cleanup step, not proof of a server-side account request. Likewise, a previously downloaded file requires attention where it was saved.

Your next step is to identify the correct account and gather the minimum records you need. Only then open the documented removal route and decide whether to confirm.
