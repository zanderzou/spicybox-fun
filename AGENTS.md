# SpicyBox Guide decisions

- Astro static output with Markdown content collections; deploy `dist/client` through GitHub-connected Cloudflare Pages.
- Visual direction: original near-black AI studio UI in hot pink, magenta, and warm red, influenced by SpicyBox's product category without copying its protected logo or claiming official status.
- All imagery must show clearly adult, non-explicit subjects. Licensed mainstream swimwear is acceptable with visible credit and no-endorsement disclosure; no nudity, transparent garments, pornographic acts, or youth-coded sexual framing.
- SEO/GEO content is answer-led with original comparisons, visible authorship, review dates, primary sources, crawlable internal links, structured data, sitemap, RSS, and IndexNow.
- Treat SpicyBox as an image-to-photo/video generator, not an AI companion or chatbot.
- Put the official self-image-only rule beside upload guidance: never encourage processing another person's photo or likeness.
- Never add functional login, payment, upload, generation, or collection of visitor images.
